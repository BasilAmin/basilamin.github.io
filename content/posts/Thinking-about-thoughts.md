I have used computers for as long as I can remember. For years I wanted a place on the internet that was actually mine—not a profile inside somebody else’s product, but a space I could understand, change, break, and rebuild.

## A workshop, not a feed

Social platforms are useful for discovery. They are terrible as a durable record of unfinished ideas, technical failures, project decisions, and the strange details that become valuable six months later.

This site is where I can show what I built, write down what I learned, and keep things that do not fit neatly into a post. Some entries will be polished. Others will be field notes written while the evidence is still fresh.

## Why build it myself

The site is intentionally small. Markdown becomes static HTML through a Node script I can read from top to bottom. There is no database and no frontend framework to maintain. That constraint makes the system easier to trust and harder to abandon.

The design follows the same rule: structure before decoration. The interesting part should be the work.
